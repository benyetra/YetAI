# League Vault — Product Requirements (Commercial Summary)

League Vault turns years of fantasy football history into a dedicated league website: standings, head-to-head results, draft recaps, transaction logs, and trophy-case records. Target customers are long-running redraft and dynasty leagues that want a permanent, shareable home for their lore without manual spreadsheet maintenance.

**Value proposition:** One-time ingest from Sleeper or ESPN, then a branded public site the league can link from group chat forever.

**Pilot:** Two leagues (Sleeper Mike's Hard + ESPN 838295) prove ingest fidelity and read-path latency before general availability.

**Monetization (future):** Per-league hosting fee or commissioner subscription; free tier with YetAI branding.

See the pilot PRD for schema, phases, and engineering status.
